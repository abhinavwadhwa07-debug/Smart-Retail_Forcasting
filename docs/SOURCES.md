# Sources and resources


## Real data to replace the sample

- **UCI Online Retail II** — UK online retailer transactions, Dec 2009 to Dec 2011; the dataset the repo's loader was written for  
  https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii
- **M5 Forecasting (Kaggle)** — Walmart daily unit sales with prices and calendar events; the standard retail forecasting benchmark  
  https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/m5-forecasting-accuracy
- **Corporación Favorita (Kaggle)** — Ecuador grocery chain sales with a perishable flag, promotions, holidays and oil prices  
  https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/favorita-grocery-sales-forecasting
- **Rossmann Store Sales (Kaggle)** — Daily store sales with promotions and school / state holidays  
  https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rossmann-store-sales
- **Open Food Facts** — Open product database: brands, categories, packaging; useful for a product master  
  https://world.openfoodfacts.org/data
- **Open Government Data Platform India** — Indian open data, including commodity price series  
  https://data.gov.in

## Methods and reading

- **Hyndman & Athanasopoulos, Forecasting: Principles and Practice (3rd ed.)** — free online textbook; baselines, evaluation, hierarchical forecasting  
  https://otexts.com/fpp3/
- **Makridakis, Spiliotis & Assimakopoulos (2022), The M5 accuracy competition: results, findings and conclusions** — International Journal of Forecasting 38(4)
- **Ke et al. (2017), LightGBM: a highly efficient gradient boosting decision tree** — NeurIPS 30; docs for the Tweedie and quantile objectives  
  https://lightgbm.readthedocs.io
- **Croston (1972); Syntetos, Boylan & Croston (2005)** — intermittent demand forecasting and demand classes (ADI, CV-squared)
- **Lundberg & Lee (2017), A unified approach to interpreting model predictions** — NeurIPS 30; SHAP explanations  
  https://shap.readthedocs.io
- **UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024** — 1.05 billion tonnes of food wasted in 2022; retail 12%  
  https://www.unep.org/resources/publication/food-waste-index-report-2024
